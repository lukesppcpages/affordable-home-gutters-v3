Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Clogged gutters are more than just an annoyance. Water flowing over your old gutters can cause serious water damage to your roof, foundation, siding and landscaping. Water damage to your home can be very expensive to fix. The Leafguard clog free guarantee eliminates the worry and hassle of clogged gutters.

Leafguard’s proprietary one-piece design utilizes the simple but powerful principle of liquid adhesion to draw only rainwater into the gutter while leaves and all other types of debris roll off. Learn more about how Leafguard Gutters work.

Yes, the Leafguard gutter system is designed to keep out pine needles, leaves, and other types of debris, covered by our no-clog guarantee.

Yes, Leafguard gutters are fully guaranteed to never clog with leaves, pine needles, or any other type of debris as long as you own your home, or we will clean or repair it for free.

Leafguard is a seamless one-piece aluminum gutter that uses thick .032 aluminum. It will never rust or deteriorate like some galvanized steel gutters. Additionally, our ScratchGuard® paint finish won’t chip, peel, crack, rot, or rust like many other gutter systems on the market.

Yes, Leafguard gutters have been tested and can handle up to 32 inches of rain per hour, which is over three times the heaviest rainfall ever recorded by the US Weather Bureau.

Leafguard gutters are a seamless one-piece gutter system that is custom fit by our professional and licensed installers on-site to the exact specifications of your home. Our on-site gutter machine cuts and fits the gutter system to the exact length and size of your home to ensure the perfect fit.

Leafguard’s one-piece gutter system attaches to your fascia board, not under your shingles, thus never voiding your roof warranty. Also, its protective baffle eliminates the possibility of debris entering the gutter from behind – a common problem with multi-piece gutters and leaf shedding covers.
